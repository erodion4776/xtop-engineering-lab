Single-degree-of-freedom free vibration

Uses ωn = √(k/m), fn = ωn/(2π), and damping ratio ζ = c/(2√(km)). The plotted response is the underdamped/critical/overdamped displacement after an initial displacement with zero initial velocity.

Stress-life (Basquin) fatigue estimate

Estimates cycles to failure using σa = σ′f(2Nf)^b. Inputs must be consistent with a material fatigue-strength coefficient and exponent from a suitable S–N data source. This is a simplified estimate, not a safe-life certification.

Enter fatigue inputs.

The simple Basquin equation does not account for mean-stress correction, variable-amplitude loading, corrosion, surface finish, size effects, notch sensitivity, or scatter unless reflected in the supplied data.
